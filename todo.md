## 1. Problem statement — TODAY (20 min)

One sentence. One named type of person, one specific pain. No "app" or "platform".

Draft to edit or replace:

> A homeowner in Maynooth whose kids have just moved out would take a student for the
> term, but has never done it, doesn't know what's involved or what it pays, and isn't
> looking anywhere — so nobody reaches them.

**My assumed pain points, ranked:**

1. People putting up listings are new to process and dont know where to start or maybe not even yet considering
2. Students see a lack of accomidation in general or surfacing these near accomidation
3. No easy site for both parties to be involved in that is frictionless and makes sense

**Riskiest assumption:**

> ~~Being first to app market.~~ Not the risk — Daft and Roomgo already have apps, and
> a cheap build is cheap for everyone. First-to-app is not a moat.
>
> **The real bet is pain point #1: can never-hosted homeowners actually be activated?**
> Everything rests on that. No listing count can answer it — only conversations can.
