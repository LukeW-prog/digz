# Irish Student Digs & Rent-a-Room Market Assessment (2026)

## 1. Executive Summary
The Irish student accommodation market in 2026 is characterized by a structural supply-demand imbalance, with a national bed shortfall estimated between **25,000 and 48,000 units** [1][2]. While Purpose-Built Student Accommodation (PBSA) and traditional rentals are increasingly constrained by the **Residential Tenancies Act 2026** (introducing rolling 6-year tenancies and 2% rent caps), the "Rent-a-Room" (digs) sector remains a critical, unregulated safety valve.

**Verdict:** There is significant room for a new entrant. The market is currently bifurcated between the "giant" generalist (Daft.ie) and fragmented, dated niche players (Digs.ie, Studentpad). **myroom.ie** is the most modern threat but lacks liquidity (approx. 60 live listings). The single strongest wedge for a new mobile-first app is **"Frictionless Trust & Verification"** — specifically bridging the "safety gap" for homeowners who fear the hassle and risk of taking in a stranger, while offering students a verified, mobile-first booking experience that incumbents currently lack.

## 2. Competitor Deep-Dive: myroom.ie
*Investigated on Aug 3, 2026*

### Feature Inventory & Walkthrough
*   **Core Flow:** Modern, mobile-responsive web platform focusing on "Community Tags" (e.g., LGBTQIA+ Friendly, Quiet Household, Near University).
*   **Seeker Profiles:** Allows students to create detailed "Room Seeker Profiles" for €1, which landlords can browse.
*   **Verification:** Identity verification offered as a paid service.
*   **Monetization:** One-time €4.99 fee for room posters; €1 for seeker profiles. No subscription or commission.
*   **Student Rent Checker:** A data-driven tool using RTB/ESRI Rent Index data (2020-2025). It estimates room costs by dividing whole-property rent by 3. While clever, it is **estimated data**, not real-time listing data [3].

## 4. Market Sizing & Assumptions
*   **Total Students:** ~260,000+ enrolled in third-level education [1].
*   **Accommodation Need:** ~45-50% of students require accommodation away from home (~120k-130k students).
*   **The Gap:** 
    *   Dublin: ~34,300 beds shortfall [2].
    *   Galway: ~10,800 beds shortfall [2].
    *   National: ~25k-30k (GSA) to 48k (Raysolute) beds [1][5].
*   **Pricing (2026):**
    *   Dublin: €700–€1,300 / month (Room only).
    *   Cork/Galway: €450–€950 / month.
*   **Seasonality:** Demand peaks sharply in **August/September** (CAO offers). Supply activity peaks in **May/June** as students leave and homeowners re-evaluate.

## 5. Demand & Supply Evidence (Unmet Needs)

### Supply-Side (Homeowners)
1.  **Tax Confusion (High):** Despite the €14,000 tax-free relief, many fear "getting into the system" or losing Mortgage Interest Relief (TRS) [6].
2.  **Safety/Privacy (High):** "Who is this person in my kitchen?" Lack of robust, trust-based vetting beyond a basic ID check.
3.  **Hassle (Medium):** Managing dozens of WhatsApp/Daft messages is overwhelming for older homeowners.

### Demand-Side (Students)
1.  **Ghosting (High):** Students report sending 50+ applications on Daft.ie with zero replies.
2.  **Scams (High):** Deposit scams are rampant on Facebook groups.
3.  **Unregulated Status (Medium):** No RTB protection means students can be evicted with "reasonable notice" (often 1 week) [7].

## 6. Differentiated Product Concepts

### Concept A: "The Verified Host" (Safety Wedge)
*   **Target:** Homeowners aged 50+ with empty nests.
*   **Core Wedge:** In-person or video-verified "Host Badges" and student "Background Checks" (Uni enrollment verification).
*   **Moat:** Incumbents (Daft) are too big to verify individual hosts; niche players lack the tech stack.

### Concept C: "The Buddy-Up Mobile App" (Social Wedge)
*   **Target:** First-year students and international students.
*   **Core Wedge:** Tinder-style matching for roommates *before* finding a room, then applying as a "verified duo."
*   **Moat:** myroom.ie has "seeker profiles," but no real-time chat or matching algorithm.

---

## 7. Kill Criteria (Reasons NOT to Build)
1.  **Institutional Capture:** If universities successfully build enough PBSA (Purpose-Built Student Accommodation) to close the gap by 2028, the "digs" market will collapse to a tiny niche.
2.  **Regulatory Shift:** If the government extends the **Residential Tenancies Act** to include "Rent-a-Room" (lodgers), the supply will vanish overnight as homeowners flee the "tenant for life" risk.
3.  **Liquidity Death Spiral:** If you cannot reach >200 listings in Dublin within 3 months, the "Daft network effect" will keep students on the larger platforms despite the poor UX.
