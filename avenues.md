# Avenues — what we could be better at

Proposals, not facts. Evidence for each is in research.md.

---

## The one thing

**A digs search that works the way digs actually work, on a phone.**

Three parts, all small:

1. **Digs-native filters.** Mon–Fri or full week. Meals included. Term-time
   dates. Owner-occupied. Nobody has these. Daft has none of them, verified
   directly.
2. **Walk time to campus.** Not "5km radius". "12 minutes' walk." Standard
   on Zillow and OnTheMarket for years. No Irish site does it. Maynooth is
   a walking town, so this is the single most useful filter there.
3. **Still available.** Show when a listing was posted and when the host
   last confirmed it. Grey out stale ones. No Irish site shows a posting
   date at all.

That is the whole product. It is not clever. It is just the first time
anyone has built search for this specific thing.

---

## Why this gap exists and stays open

- **Daft can't.** They are a generalist portal. Digs is a rounding error
  inside 2,722 share listings. Adding meal plans and term dates to a
  national property portal is not worth their time.
- **Studentpad can't quickly.** It is 25-year-old UK software licensed to
  100+ institutions in four countries. Irish digs quirks are not on their
  roadmap.
- **myroom, Digs.ie, CollegeCribs won't.** No app, small teams, web only.

Nobody in Irish digs has a native mobile app. That is the state of the art
we are competing against.

## Ranked avenues

### 1. Digs-native filters
Mon–Fri, meals, term dates, owner-occupied.
**Evidence:** Daft audit shows zero of these. Studentpad mixes digs with
apartments and house shares.
**Effort:** low. **Risk:** copyable, but nobody has in 20 years.

### 2. Walk and cycle time to campus
**Evidence:** standard abroad, absent in Ireland. MU digs cluster in
Maynooth town and surrounding villages where walk time varies hugely.
**Effort:** low, one mapping API. **Risk:** low.

### 3. Freshness and "still available"
Host confirms weekly. Listing greys out if not.
**Evidence:** no Irish site shows posting dates. 70 applications produced
7 viewings for one student. Facebook groups are full of dead reposts.
**Effort:** low. **Risk:** needs host engagement to work.

### 4. Scam-safe by design
Never pay before viewing, stated in the flow. No off-platform deposit
requests. Report button.
**Evidence:** 230 Garda reports and €400,000 lost to end July 2026, peaking
Aug–Oct. All three named scam patterns involve money moving before viewing.
**Effort:** low, mostly design and copy. **Risk:** low. Strong marketing story.

### 5. The vetting double standard
MU sends an assessor to visit international host families, and explicitly
does not vet landlords for everyone else. ISI Garda-vets its hosts.
**Opportunity:** offer the international-grade standard to ordinary students.
**Effort:** high, it is operational not technical. **Risk:** high, this is
a services business, not an app. Park it, but it is the strongest long-term
differentiator anyone has left on the table.

### 6. International students
Told not to book remotely, but many must.
**Evidence:** standard advice is book only PBSA from abroad. Digs host
families run €400–€600/month with meals.
**Opportunity:** video walkthroughs, verified host, safe remote commit.
**Effort:** medium. **Risk:** medium. Depends on avenue 5 to be credible.

### 7. Aggregation
Pull Studentpad, Daft and Facebook posts into one search.
**Upside:** solves cold start instantly. Day one we have more digs in
Maynooth than anyone.
**Risk:** legal and technical. Scraping terms, breakage, no host
relationship. Worth a serious look, not a casual one.

### 8. Just be the polished app
Genuinely an option. Every competitor is web-only, dated, or generalist.
A fast, clean, mobile-first digs app would be the best thing in the market
on execution alone, before any feature.
**Reality check:** polish is not defensible. But it does not need to be
defensible to win a town. It needs to be better than a Facebook group.

### 9. Mon–Fri workers
Digs.ie targets this and is dated. Contract and rotation workers near
Maynooth want the same weekday room.
**Effort:** near zero, it is a filter we already have.
**Upside:** demand outside the Sept spike, which smooths the whole year.

### 10. January intake
Semester two and Erasmus. Underserved, less competition, and a real
deadline that is not next September.
**Use:** the natural first live test.

## What to ignore for now

Roommate matching, payments and deposits, reviews and ratings, multi-city,
host acquisition campaigns, and verification as a paid product. All are
features to add once search works, not reasons to exist.

---

## Honest summary

The defensible answer is avenue 5, vetting, and it is an operations
business we are not set up to run yet.

The winnable answer is avenues 1, 2 and 3 shipped well. It is a better
search, not a new idea, and being first to build it properly for Irish
digs is a real if temporary edge.

Avenue 7, aggregation, decides whether we launch with 80 listings or 5.
That is the biggest open question.
