Information

Guides for everyone involved.

Your rights in digs

Digs are a licence, not a tenancy — you're not covered by the RTB. Know what notice you can be given before you move in.

What to check first

Bills included? Kitchen access? Weekends allowed? Ask before you pay anything, and never pay a deposit before viewing.

Rent-a-Room relief

You can earn up to €14,000 a year tax-free letting a room in your own home. It doesn't affect your mortgage interest relief.

A room in Maynooth typically earns
€450–€900

per month. Over an academic year that's roughly €4,000–€8,000 — inside the tax-free limit.

Term-time or Mon–Fri

You choose. Many hosts let the room September to May only, or weekdays only, and keep their weekends.

Digs for people working locally

Contract or rotation work nearby? A weekday room is often cheaper and closer than a full tenancy.

What hosts want to know

Where you work, how long you need the room, and whether you're home at weekends.
